Kash Kittens

Follow these steps to install the Modrinth App and add the Kash Kittens modpack. The modpack used for this server is available in the Discord.

  1. 1

    Download the Modrinth App

    Open the official Modrinth App page, choose your operating system, and run the installer.

    Download Modrinth App ↗
  2. 2

    Sign in to Minecraft

    Open Modrinth and sign in with the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.

  3. 3

    Download the modpack from Discord

    Open the server Discord, find the latest Kash Kittens modpack post, and download the file ending in .mrpack. Keep it as an .mrpack—do not unzip it.

  4. 4

    Import the modpack

    In Modrinth, open Library, press the + button, choose From file, and select the downloaded .mrpack. Wait while everything installs.

  5. 5

    Launch the pack

    Open the new Kash Kittens instance and press Play. The first launch may take longer than normal.

◆
Allocate RAM before playing

This is a large modpack. We recommend 8 GB; use at least 6 GB when possible.

Performance setupHow to allocate RAMOpen tutorialClose tutorial
  1. Go to Library and open the Kash Kittens instance.
  2. Click the instance Settings cog in the top-right.
  3. Select Java and memory.
  4. Enable Custom memory allocation.
  5. Set Allocated Memory to 8192 MiB for 8 GB, or 6144 MiB for 6 GB.
  6. Return to the instance and restart it before pressing Play.

Open Parties and Claims 0.30.3 lets friends share a party, protect chunks, control who may interact inside them, and optionally keep claimed chunks loaded. Command suggestions appear when you press Tab.

Open the menu

Press the apostrophe key '. You can change this in Minecraft's keybind settings.

Claim on the map

Open Xaero's World Map with M, then right-click or right-drag chunks.

Know the limit

Your claim and forceload totals depend on this server's settings.

How parties work
  1. Create a party with /oparties create.
  2. Invite friends. They can click the chat invitation or join with its party ID.
  3. The owner can promote members to moderator. Moderators can invite and remove members.
  4. Enable party claiming before claiming land that the whole party should share.
  5. Party allies may receive access according to the claim's permission settings; non-allies means everyone else.

A party is identified by its owner's UUID. If the owner leaves, ownership must first be transferred or the party destroyed.

How claims work
  1. Stand inside the chunk you want, or select chunks on Xaero's World Map.
  2. Choose whether the claim belongs to you or your party. Party mode requires the correct party rank or permission.
  3. Claiming protects the full 16×16 chunk from breaking, placing, containers, mobs, and other actions according to its settings.
  4. Use the claim settings screen to choose what party members, allies, and non-allies may do.
  5. Forceload only the chunks that truly need to run while nobody is nearby; it is separate from protection.

Map shortcut: press M, right-click a chunk for actions, or hold and drag the right mouse button across several chunks. The server can change claim limits and permissions.
